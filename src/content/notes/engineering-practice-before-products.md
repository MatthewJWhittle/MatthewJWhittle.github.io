You can buy cloud GPUs in an afternoon. You cannot buy a shared habit of pull requests.

At SLR I worked as an adjacent technical lead to an existing geospatial development group — coaching Git, review, tests and deployment rather than managing the team by line. The point was not bureaucracy; it was making the next piece of software reviewable by someone who did not write it.

What changed my thinking: cultural change through influence is real work, and it should be described that way — not as “I owned the team.”
