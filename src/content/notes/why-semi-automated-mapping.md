Fully automatic habitat maps look impressive in demos. In assessment work, a wrong polygon still becomes someone’s liability.

The useful shape of the product is a **proposal**: tiled inference, vectorised patches, clear uncertainty, and a GIS/ecology review step. That is slower to market than a magic map, and much more honest about geography the model has not seen.

What changed my thinking: treating QA and training-data hygiene as product features, not QA theatre after the model ships.
