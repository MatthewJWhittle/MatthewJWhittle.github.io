Reducing thousands of clips to a few hundred for specialists is a success metric. Calling that “AI identified the species” is a different claim entirely.

On the camera-trap study I led, triage meant experts still verified what mattered. The model earned its keep by deciding **what deserved attention**.

What changed my thinking: product copy should match the evaluation. If you measured triage, say triage.
